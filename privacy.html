Elderthrone Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Elderthrone is a life counter for tabletop card games. It is built to work without collecting any information about you.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your current game (player names, life totals, counters, commander names and settings) on your device so you can resume where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
