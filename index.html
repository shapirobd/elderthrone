Elderthrone Support

Elderthrone is a life counter for tabletop card games, with game modes for Commander, 1v1, Brawl, Oathbreaker and Two-Headed Giant.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas: elderthrone.app@gmail.com

Quick help

  • Tap the − or + half of a player's area to change life by 1. Hold to change it by 10.
  • The CMD button in a player's area shows and sets commander damage from each opponent's commander.
  • The slider button opens counters, status (monarch, initiative) and the player's name, color and commander.
  • The center button opens the game menu: new game, restart, players and layout, dice, first player and history.
